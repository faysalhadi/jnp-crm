STEP 1 — SOURCING SECTION
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
 
Insert a new section DIRECTLY UNDER "ACT NOW". It must never be collapsed —
this is live work against a two-day clock.
 
  Label:  SOURCING
  Dot:    amber
  Members: every customer with at least one deal at stage 'sourcing'
 
  Sort: oldest sourcing_started_at first.
 
  On each card, a chip from sourcingAge(deal):
      level 'ok'    →  "day 1"           amber
      level 'warn'  →  "day 2"           amber, stronger
      level 'late'  →  "day 3 — close it out"   red
 
  The reason line reads:
      `Looking for ${brand} ${model} × ${quantity}`
 
Faisal loses the customer to another supplier after about two days. That is
what these thresholds encode — do not change them.

STEP 2 — PARKED SECTION
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
 
At the VERY BOTTOM of the queue, below every other section:
 
      👁  PARKED                                        14 ▸
 
  · Collapsed by default. Tap the header to expand.
  · Keep expanded/collapsed in component state only — do not persist it.
  · Members: customers with at least one deal at stage 'parked',
    EXCLUDING anyone already rendered in a higher section (no duplicates).
  · Sort by parked_at, OLDEST FIRST — the longest-ignored surface first.
  · Reason line by parked_reason:
        price_too_high    → `Offered ${money}/unit · ${n} weeks ago`
        not_sourced       → `Couldn't find ${brand} ${model}`
        bought_elsewhere  → `Bought elsewhere · ${n} weeks ago`
        went_quiet        → `Went quiet · ${n} weeks ago`
        not_needed_now    → `Not needed yet · ${n} weeks ago`
  · Each card shows quantity and the model, same as any other card.
  · An "Unpark" action on each card calls unparkDeal(dealId, 'new_inquiry').

STEP 3 — ROLE SCOPING  (this is the owner's answer)
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
 
Use the real ProfileContext API found in step 0.
 
  SALESPERSON → sees only deals where created_by is himself, in both the
                SOURCING and PARKED sections.
  OWNER/MANAGER → sees everyone's, by default.
 
Do NOT build a separate owner screen. Role sets the default scope.
 
3a. OWNER FILTER ROW
    When the viewer is owner or manager, render a compact filter row at the
    top of the expanded PARKED section:
 
        [ Everyone ]  [ Ahmed ]  [ Bilal ]  [ Kashif ]  [ Faisal ]
 
    Chips, horizontally scrollable, "Everyone" selected by default. Names
    come from profiles where active = true. Filters the parked list by
    created_by. Hidden entirely for a salesperson.
 
3b. OWNER SUMMARY STRIP
    Above that filter row, when the viewer is owner or manager, a compact
    summary — one line per active salesperson:
 
        Ahmed     14 parked · 620 units · avg 22d
        Bilal      6 parked · 180 units · avg 8d
        Kashif    11 parked · 430 units · avg 17d
 
    · units = sum of deals.quantity across that person's parked deals
    · avg = mean days since parked_at
    · Tapping a row applies that person's filter (same as the chip)
    · Sort by parked count descending
    · Use tabular-nums so the columns line up
    · Keep it visually quiet — small text, muted. It is reference, not a
      dashboard.
 
    This is the view Faisal asked for twice: what each of his four people is
    sitting on. If one agent parks twice what another does, that shows here.

STEP 4 — THE PARK ACTION
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
 
If step 0 found no Park button, add one now.
 
On every OPEN deal (any stage except closed and parked), in the client
detail / right panel, a secondary "Park" button. Opens a bottom sheet:
 
  Title: "Park this one"
  Sub:   "<client> · <brand> <model> × <qty>"
 
  Five large tap targets from PARK_REASONS:
      💰 Price too high        → reveals a REQUIRED
                                 "What did he offer per unit" field
      🔍 Couldn't source it
      🏃 Bought elsewhere
      🤐 Went quiet
      📅 Not needed now
 
  Then a summary block before confirming:
      ✓ Leaves the queue · follow-up closed
      ✓ <n> units released back to free      (only when a hold exists)
      ✓ Joins <brand> <model> parked demand
      ✓ Comes back when stock lands or his price clears
 
  Confirm calls parkService.parkDeal(dealId, { reason, targetUnitPrice }).
  On success, refresh the customer list.
